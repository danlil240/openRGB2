---------------------------------------------------------------------
Excluded components (project packaging rules)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The following are deliberately NOT shipped: legacy OpenRGB effect
plugins, the historical WinRing0-based HardwareSync dependency, and
the installed L-Connect yuz.dll. The staging script verifies their
absence and fails the package if any appear.
